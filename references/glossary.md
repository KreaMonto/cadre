# CADRE — Technical Glossary

This glossary provides canonical translations of key statistical and analytical
terms. When producing reports in a non-English language, use these terms
consistently. Where a term is commonly used untranslated in a language's
scientific literature, that usage is noted.

Format: **English term** | *translation* | notes

## Notes for Contributors

When adding a new language:
1. Prioritize terms that appear in CADRE stage templates and formula blocks.
2. Note when the English term is used untranslated in the scientific literature
   of that language — forcing a translation where none is used in practice
   makes reports read as unnatural.
3. Verify translations against published textbooks or peer-reviewed articles
   in that language, not just dictionary lookups.
